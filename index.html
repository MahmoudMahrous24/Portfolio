S-Report AI: Universal Urban Intelligence & Disaster Management

A hybrid multimodal spatial AI system engineered for real-time disaster detection and automated emergency response. Uses CLIP zero-shot classification fused with ConvNeXt-Tiny for rapid hazard identification. Integrates BLIP semantic scene captioning, YOLOv8 instance segmentation, and MiDaS monocular depth estimation for 3D spatial intelligence. Coupled with an algorithmic weighted dispatch decision engine and production-grade FastAPI + Docker backend.

DermaAssist AI — Bilingual Clinical Decision Support

Award-winning bilingual (Arabic/English) clinical decision support platform for dermatology, strictly grounded in official World Health Organization (WHO) clinical guidelines.
